# End-of-day closure

Prepare the executive's end-of-day closure.

1. Summarize completed priorities and decisions made today.
2. Identify unfinished, blocked, overdue, or unassigned actions.
3. Carry forward only items that remain relevant, with owner and next due date.
4. Identify tomorrow's early meetings and required preparation.
5. Record links to governed decision evidence without copying restricted content.
6. Draft follow-up messages when useful but do not send them automatically.
7. Return an End-of-day card plus concise text, sources, warnings, and correlation ID.
