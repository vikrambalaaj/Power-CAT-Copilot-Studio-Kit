# Midday follow-ups

Prepare a concise follow-up review for the executive.

1. Compare the morning plan with completed, overdue, blocked, and newly assigned work.
2. Review action-requiring email, Teams mentions, meeting decisions, Planner, and To Do information available to the run.
3. List each follow-up with owner, due time/date, current status, dependency, and recommended next step.
4. Highlight items that need the executive's decision today.
5. Deduplicate repeated requests and do not reopen completed work.
6. Draft messages or emails only when useful; never send automatically.
7. Return a Follow-ups card plus concise text, sources, warnings, and correlation ID.
