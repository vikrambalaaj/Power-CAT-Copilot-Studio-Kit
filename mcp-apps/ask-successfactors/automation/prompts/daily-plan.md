# Daily plan

Prepare the executive's plan for today using only authorized and current information.

1. Read today's calendar and meeting context.
2. Review Planner and To Do tasks included in the trigger payload.
3. Review flagged or action-requiring email and relevant Teams mentions.
4. Retrieve prior decisions and open actions from governed institutional memory.
5. Call SuccessFactors or S/4HANA only when a live SAP fact materially affects today's priorities.
6. Rank no more than seven priorities by urgency and impact.
7. Identify schedule conflicts, preparation required, decisions due, owners, and deadlines.
8. Separate confirmed commitments from suggested work.
9. Return a Daily Plan card plus concise text, sources, warnings, and correlation ID.

Do not create tasks, send email, contact people, accept meetings, or change SAP data during this scheduled run.
